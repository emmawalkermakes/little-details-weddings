Little Details Weddings is an independent hobby blog about the small, personal parts of a wedding: the favours, the welcome sign, the place cards, the thank-you gifts for the people who helped. They're the details guests notice and couples remember, and they're also the bits that tend to pile up into a stressful to-do list in the final month.

It's written in Brisbane, so there's a strong focus on what works for Australian weddings: outdoor ceremonies, spring and summer heat, afternoon storms, and venues that range from wineries and beaches to backyards and halls.

## What you'll find here

- **Ideas with practical notes**: what survives heat, wind and humidity, and what to ask your venue.
- **Timelines and checklists** so personalised items are ordered early enough.
- **Budget-conscious options** alongside the special-occasion ones.
- **No fake reviews, no invented statistics**, and no pressure to buy everything.

There's a free [wedding personalisation checklist](/checklist/) you can tick off in your browser.

## Disclosure

This is a hobby blog. I enjoy making things, and now and then I may feature something I make myself. When I do, I'll say so clearly in the post. There are no sponsored posts or paid placements.
